# Delegation in Telescope

Your 首席幕僚 can hand a piece of work to one of your project 首席代理, and a 首席代理 can
open Worker threads in its project to do it. Each hand-off is a delegation (委派).

## Delegate work

In Telescope, open **对话** and pick your 首席幕僚 or a 首席代理. Say what you want done, any
constraints, and when it counts as finished. The agent delegates it and tells you in the same
chat when the work is done or has failed.

## Answer an agent's question

When an agent needs a choice or your approval, its delegation waits and the question appears
under **需要你定** at the top of the conversation page. The same question is in the **决定**
list and in the decisions screen of the mobile app. Pick an option and confirm. The answer goes
back to the agent or Worker thread that asked, and the work continues. A decision cannot be
taken back.

Questions are shared decisions, so any workspace member can answer one. Approving a request does
not change the agent's powers; change those in **My Teams**.

## Cancel a delegation

Below **需要你定**, **委派** lists the selected agent's open delegations, with a link to each
Worker thread. **取消** stops the delegation and everything it handed on. The threads are kept.
To pick the work up again, delegate it again.
